# Contributors

UI Iceberg is created by [@cyrillesaxo](https://github.com/cyrillesaxo) and published by **Dodo LLC**.

External contributors will be acknowledged here after merged contributions. Credit should reflect the kind of contribution rather than a quantity leaderboard.

Useful contribution categories include:

- journey scenario packs,
- testing-tool adapters,
- reproducible false-convergence benchmark fixtures,
- documentation and examples,
- bug reports with minimal reproductions,
- research references and counterexamples.

See the repository's `good first issue` and `help wanted` issues for current opportunities.
